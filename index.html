Project charter

Adaptive microscopy is the first test case.

The working question is whether a low-cost imaging rig can estimate surface-defect density to within 10% of an exhaustive scan while imaging at least 50% fewer fields of view. A robot is optional infrastructure; calibrated evidence is the core contribution.

Instrument
Fixed optical camera, controlled lighting, calibrated XY positioning
Safe samples
Reference targets and nonhazardous polymer or coated coupons
Ground truth
Exhaustive raster scan, resolution target, and repeat measurements
Decision
Stop, rescan, recalibrate, or select the next informative region
01

Calibrate first

Measure resolution, scale, illumination stability, repeatability, and drift before training a model.

02

Keep uncertainty

Return confidence and failure flags with every result—not only a label or defect count.

03

Earn the robot

Add motion only when it improves coverage, repeatability, throughput, or measurement quality.

Candidate modalities

One first. Two held in reserve.

The first modality is a recommendation, not a purchase decision. Each option still needs sample access and calibration review.

01 / OPTICALRECOMMENDED

Adaptive microscopy

Map defects or texture across a surface, choosing new fields of view from uncertainty and spatial variation.

  • Calibrate scale, resolution, distortion, illumination, and focus
  • Use safe reference targets and surface coupons
  • Ground truth with a complete raster scan
02 / SPECTRALCANDIDATE

Reflectance sensing

Compare visible/NIR response across coatings, finishes, or known reference patches under fixed geometry.

  • Calibrate dark signal, white reference, channel response, and drift
  • Requires stable illumination and reference standards
  • Useful as a second modality after imaging works
03 / ELECTRICALCANDIDATE

Impedance mapping

Measure low-voltage frequency response of conductive films, devices, or known RC standards.

  • Calibrate open, short, and reference loads
  • Requires repeatable contacts and approved samples
  • Strong fit only if lab access provides a real question
